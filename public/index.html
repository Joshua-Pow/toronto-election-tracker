        Unofficial results from the City of Toronto media results feed. The City publishes about once a minute. This page reads the file one second after each publication, and every 20 seconds until the file starts changing. Official results are certified by the City Clerk after election night. Ward boundaries: Toronto Open Data, City Wards. Map tiles © Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors. All times Eastern (ET).

        What do these numbers mean?
        • Polls reporting: voting subdivisions whose counts the City has received, out of 1,431 for Mayor.
        • Share: a candidate's votes divided by all votes counted so far for that race. On a ward card, the percentage next to the leader is the leader's share in that ward.
        • Margin: votes (and percentage points) between first and second place right now.
        • Advance-vote results are released after polls close at 8 PM ET, so early totals can swing.
        • This page does not project a winner. It only shows what the City has counted.
